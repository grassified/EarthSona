# EarthSona roadmap

## Shipped

- Responsive Player, Explore, Lab, Library, Studio, and Settings in English and Turkish.
- Four image datasets, aligned globe and 2D views where supported, and a 2D view for GEDI Forest Height.
- Motif and Flow playback, shared Player/Lab transport and scan speed, and Light, Dark, and Black themes with custom accents.
- Local Studio editing and preview, WAV export, and WebM / Opus export where the browser supports recording in that format.

## Verification still open

- Exercise touch, orientation changes, and playback on a physical phone.
- Test announcements, focus order, and playback controls with screen readers.
- Listen on a mono speaker for the Night Lights contours, Ocean Currents Ambient Flow continuity, and Studio export playback.

## Optional post-hackathon work

- Review one additional dataset at a time, with its provenance, alignment, and contrasting preset samples in the same PR.
- Evaluate MP3 export alongside the existing formats.
